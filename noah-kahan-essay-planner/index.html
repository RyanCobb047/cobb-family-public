Name: ________________________     Date: ________________________

Assignment: Write an essay about a prominent person

Big idea: Good writing is not just facts. It is facts + opinion + reflection.

1. Why did you choose Noah Kahan?

Write what made you pick him.

2. Quick facts about Noah Kahan

Where is he from?
What does he do?
What kind of music does he make?
How did he become well known?

3. What did you notice about him?

Circle in your mind what fits best, then explain:

honest • shy • thoughtful • brave • relatable • emotional • hardworking • real

4. What stood out to you most?

What moment, idea, or feeling from the documentary stuck with you?

5. Why is Noah Kahan a prominent person?

This will help you build your main idea.

6. Build your thesis statement

Use this sentence frame:
Noah Kahan is a prominent person because ____________________________________________.

7. Organize your essay

Introduction

Hook: Write an interesting opening sentence.
Introduce Noah Kahan.
Write your thesis.

Body Paragraph 1 — Background

What are the most important facts readers should know about him?

Body Paragraph 2 — Why he matters

What makes him important, interesting, or different?

Body Paragraph 3 — Your reflection

What do you admire, notice, or feel about Noah Kahan?

Conclusion

Restate your main idea in a new way.
Write a strong final sentence (clincher).
